How it works

AssemblyAI Dictation returns both the verbatim transcript and an LLM rewrite. Redline aligns them with a deterministic checker — no second model grading the first.

01

Speak

Record from the browser as 16-bit PCM. Space starts and stops when the app has focus.

02

Compare

Negations, numbers, and invented names are checked against the verbatim string.

03

Copy with a guard

High-severity findings require a second confirm before the rewrite hits the clipboard.
